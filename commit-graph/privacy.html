Commit Graph: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-27

Summary

Commit Graph is a watch face for Wear OS. It collects no personal data, stores no personal data, and shares no personal data.

Data shown on the watch face

The watch face shows the time, date, battery level, step goal progress, and whatever complications you choose, such as heart rate or notifications. The watch's operating system provides these values to the watch face on your device. They are shown on screen only. They are never recorded or transmitted.

No code, no network

Commit Graph is built with the Watch Face Format. It contains no executable code, has no internet access, and includes no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Children

The watch face does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
